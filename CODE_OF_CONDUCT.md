# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, the
maintainers and contributors of `termpdf-rs` pledge to make
participation in our project and community a respectful experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Behavior that is unacceptable:

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (physical or electronic
  address, etc.) without explicit permission.
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull
requests, commit messages, project-affiliated chat channels, and
public spaces when an individual is representing the project. It
also applies to actions taken outside these spaces that have a
direct, demonstrable negative impact on the project's community.

## Enforcement

Reports of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior
should be sent to **amanagrawal22222@gmail.com**.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a
response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances. The maintainer team is obligated to maintain
confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by
other members of the project's leadership.
